How to play

Drag on the glass to scrape off the frost. Every drag is one swipe, and you only get five.

Each swipe can only go so far before the scraper is full, so plan your lines.

Clear enough glass to pass. Clear more for three stars, and look for hidden things under the frost.

Thick ice needs two passes. Stickers can't be scraped.
