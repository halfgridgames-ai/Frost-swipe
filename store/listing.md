Brr! The windows have frozen over. Grab your scraper and clear the glass, but careful: you only get FIVE swipes.

Every drag is one swipe, and your scraper fills up as it goes, so plan your lines. Scrape the frost from village windows, car windscreens, phone screens, ship portholes, train windows, snow globes and more, and watch the cosy winter scene appear.

❄️ FIVE SWIPES, ONE WINDOW
Simple to learn: drag to scrape. Tricky to master: clear enough glass to pass, clear more for three stars.

🧊 SO SATISFYING
Crunchy frost, squeaky ice, wiping fog, sliding snow. Every surface scrapes and sounds different.

🔍 HIDDEN SURPRISES
Little things are hidden under the frost. Find them all for bonus coins.

🌨️ ENDLESS VARIETY
Thick ice that needs two passes, foggy mirrors, snowy globes, muddy windscreens, shaped windows and stickers that won't budge.

📅 DAILY WINDOW
A new frozen window every day. Keep your streak going and share your score.

🖼️ COLLECT POSTCARDS
Clear a new scene to add it to your postcard album, and unlock new scrapers.

Boosters help when a window is stubborn: an extra swipe, a wide blade or a warm breath to melt the edges.
